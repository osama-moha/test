The Assessment Gap

A sales quotation is not a site assessment.

A security proposal is stronger when it starts with the site conditions, the way access is managed and the points where control is breaking down.

Equipment Quotation

  • Starts with an equipment or staffing list
  • Focuses on what can be supplied
  • May recommend products before the full site context is known
  • Produces a quotation

Urban Eye Site Security Review

  • Starts with site conditions and operating risks
  • Identifies where control is failing and why
  • Documents findings before recommendations are made
  • Produces an assessment basis and prioritised next actions

What The Review Covers

The review turns site observations into an action plan.

Each review follows a structured methodology to evaluate physical vulnerabilities and access routines.

Site Survey

Inspect the site before recommending a solution.

We review entry points, perimeter condition, lighting, vulnerable areas, access routines and the movement of people, vehicles or materials.

Written Findings

Turn observations into a usable record.

The review documents the main control gaps, relevant observations and the issues requiring management attention.

Priority Actions

Address the highest exposure first.

Recommendations identify immediate controls, longer-term improvements and the services or equipment appropriate for the site.

FAQs

Common questions

Clear answers on how a Site Security Review helps you plan your protection.

What is included in a Site Security Review?

An Urban Eye specialist inspects your property layout, perimeter points, access routines, lighting, and vulnerable areas, delivering documented findings and prioritized recommendations.

Is a Site Security Review suitable for residential properties?

Yes. We conduct reviews for private homes, apartment compounds, residential estates, as well as commercial premises, warehouses, and active construction sites.

How does a review differ from a sales quotation?

A sales quotation focuses on equipment lists and hardware costs. A Site Security Review identifies where control is failing on site before any equipment or personnel are recommended.

What if I need a formal written report?

The Corporate Risk Audit is a paid assessment that produces a written risk register with severity ratings, photo evidence, and a phased remediation plan. The fee is quoted by site scope and credited in full against implementation.
