Who We Partner With

Built for organizations that answer for many properties.

Managing security property by property creates inconsistent standards, fragmented reporting, and unclear accountability. A partnership consolidates all of it under one framework.

Property Managers

Uniform protection for every building you manage.

One escalation procedure, one reporting format, and one accountable provider across your portfolio. Each property receives its own risk review, deployment record, and incident log, consolidated into a monthly portfolio summary for owners and boards.

Developers

Security specified before handover, not after the first incident.

Urban Eye works with developers to design security into the property: access control, CCTV coverage, alarm zoning, and documented response procedures, delivered as a complete secure-handover package that raises the value of the finished development.

Construction Firms

Repeatable site protection for every project you run.

A standing framework covering gate control, material exit permits, subcontractor verification, perimeter detection, and daily reporting, deployed to each new site under pre-agreed terms instead of a new procurement cycle every project.

Short-Stay Operators

Guest-safe security across every unit you operate.

Smart access, fire alerts, panic support, and remote oversight deployed consistently across managed units, with incident documentation that protects both the operator and the property owner.

What Partners Receive

A partnership is a service standard, not a discount code.

Consolidated reporting

A monthly portfolio risk summary covering incidents, response outcomes, system health, and recurring exposure patterns across all covered properties.

Pre-agreed deployment terms

Standardized pricing, response procedures, and installation standards, so adding a new property or site does not require a new negotiation.

A single point of accountability

One dedicated contact responsible for every covered property, with documented escalation paths for managers, owners, and site teams.

Priority scheduling

Partner properties receive priority for site surveys, maintenance visits, and deployment capacity.

Start with one property.

Urban Eye will conduct a documented risk review on a single property you manage. Evaluate the report, the process, and the reporting standard before extending coverage across the portfolio.
