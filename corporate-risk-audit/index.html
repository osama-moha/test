FAQs

Common questions

Clear answers help procurement teams, property managers, and directors compare this service with a free site visit or quotation.

Is this a free quote?

No. The Corporate Risk Audit is a paid written assessment for buyers who want a documented risk report before approving implementation.

What happens after the audit?

You receive the report, review the recommendations internally, and decide whether Urban Eye should quote the approved scope. If you proceed within the stated threshold and time window, the audit fee is credited in full.

Can this be used for a construction site or remote property?

Yes. The audit is especially valuable where management needs documented visibility, a staged security plan, and stronger escalation structure across a changing or distributed site.

Is the report confidential?

Yes. The report is prepared for the named client and should be treated as confidential operational material.

Do you guarantee loss prevention?

No security approach eliminates all risk. The audit identifies vulnerabilities, prioritizes mitigations, and helps reduce preventable exposure through stronger controls, visibility, and response planning.
