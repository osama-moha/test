What You Receive

A deliverable your executive team can approve.

This is not an informal walkthrough or sales quotation. You receive a structured written report that management can review, budget against, and execute in clear commercial phases.

1. Physical Site Walkthrough

Comprehensive inspection of perimeters, gates, lighting, blind spots, loading zones, and critical asset areas.

2. Control Systems Audit

Evaluation of active guards, CCTV coverage, access routines, visitor logging, and incident escalation discipline.

3. Written Risk Register

Itemized vulnerabilities rated by severity level with photographic evidence and exact location references.

4. Phased Remediation Plan

Prioritized roadmap separating urgent high-exposure fixes from long-term capital improvements.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How is the audit fee calculated?

Fees are based on property acreage, building square footage, and site complexity. A fixed fee is confirmed in writing before scheduling.

How does the 100% fee credit work?

If you approve Urban Eye to implement any recommended guards, CCTV, alarms, or perimeter systems, the full audit fee is deducted from your implementation invoice.

What is the delivery timeline?

Standard written audit reports are completed and delivered within 5 working days of the physical site survey.

Is the audit report confidential?

Yes. All findings, severity ratings, and photo evidence belong strictly to your company and are delivered under strict confidentiality.

What is the difference between this and a Site Security Review?

The Site Security Review is a complimentary initial walkthrough. The Corporate Risk Audit is a comprehensive paid evaluation delivering a formal written risk register, severity ratings, and photo evidence for management approval.
