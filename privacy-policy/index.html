Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Urban Eye Security ("Urban Eye," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used, stored, and protected.

Information we collect

When you request a site assessment, request a package, or contact us through this website, we collect:

  • Your name and phone number (required to respond to your request)
  • Your property location, timeline, and the type of service or package you're interested in (optional, provided by you)
  • Any additional details you choose to share about your security needs

We also use Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking (via Google's gtag.js) to understand how visitors use this site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. This may involve cookies and similar technologies that collect general usage data such as pages visited and device/browser type. This data is not used to personally identify you beyond what Google's own privacy practices govern.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your site assessment or package request
  • To follow up by phone, WhatsApp, or email about your inquiry
  • To improve our services and this website
  • To meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How we share your information

Form submissions on this site are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, solely to deliver your inquiry to our team. We may also share information with subcontracted installation or monitoring partners strictly as needed to deliver the service you requested.

Data retention

We retain inquiry and customer information for as long as needed to respond to your request, deliver a service you've engaged us for, or comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data held by us. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Contact us
