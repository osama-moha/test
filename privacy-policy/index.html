Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Urban Eye Security ("Urban Eye," "we," "us") of St. Ellis, Wabera Street, Nairobi CBD, Kenya is the data controller for personal information collected through this website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used, stored, and protected.

Information we collect

When you request a site assessment, request a package, or contact us through this website, we collect:

  • Your name and phone number (required to respond to your request)
  • Your property location, timeline, and the type of service or package you're interested in (optional, provided by you)
  • Any additional details you choose to share about your security needs

We also use Google Analytics and Google Ads tracking to understand how visitors use this site and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage data such as pages visited and device or browser type. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

When you first visit this site, a cookie notice lets you accept or decline these cookies. Analytics and advertising cookies are withheld until you accept. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will show the notice again.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your site assessment or package request
  • To follow up by phone, WhatsApp, or email about your inquiry
  • To improve our services and this website
  • To meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations where applicable

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

How we share your information

Form submissions on this site are processed through Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, solely to deliver your inquiry to our team. When you complete a form, your inquiry is also passed to WhatsApp so our team can respond to you there, and its handling by WhatsApp is governed by Meta's privacy policy. We may also share information with subcontracted installation or monitoring partners strictly as needed to deliver the service you requested.

International data transfers

Formspree, Google, and WhatsApp process data on servers located outside Kenya. Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we rely on providers that maintain appropriate safeguards for personal data, in line with section 48 of the Data Protection Act, 2019.

Data retention

We retain inquiry and customer information for as long as needed to respond to your request, deliver a service you've engaged us for, or comply with legal obligations. Inquiry data that does not lead to an engagement is deleted or anonymized within 12 months of your last contact with us.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data held by us, the right to object to or restrict its processing, and the right to data portability. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your personal data has been mishandled.
